Title: CosyVoice2 bring-up (#54104): iSTFT, HiFT, Qwen2 LLM, flow decoder; streaming pipeline in progress

### Summary

TTNN bring-up of [CosyVoice2](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice) on N150 (Wormhole), for #54104. This is still a draft. It is ready for early feedback, but not for full review until the streaming pipeline lands.

### What's in this PR (`models/demos/audio/cosyvoice2/`)

| Component | Implementation | Validation (N150) |
|---|---|---|
| iSTFT (`tt/hifigan/istft.py`) | Matmul inverse DFT, overlap-add as a transposed conv, NOLA normalization | PCC ≥ 0.999 against `torch.istft` (bf16 inputs) at 256–65,537 frames (`test_istft.py`) |
| HiFT vocoder (`tt/hifigan/`) | 3-stage upsample and resblocks, ConvRNN F0 predictor, SineGen2/NSF source, STFT and iSTFT head | Real `hift.pt`: decode PCC ≥ 0.999 (fp32); F0 predictor 0.996–0.999 (`test_hift_checkpoint.py`, `test_f0_predictor_checkpoint.py`) |
| LLM (`tt/llm/`) | Qwen2-0.5B on tt_transformers, CosyVoice2 sequence assembly, prefill and decode, RAS sampling, opt-in traced decode (41 → 9.8 ms/token, matching eager bit for bit) | `test_qwen2lm*.py`, including the real fine-tuned backbone from `llm.pt` |
| Flow decoder (`tt/flow/`) | Upsample Conformer encoder with relative-position attention, CFM estimator with Euler solver and CFG (opt-in traced), `CausalMaskedDiffWithXvec` wiring; chunk-causal streaming masks and bucketing in both the encoder and the CFM | Real `flow.pt`: bucketed vs exact-length streaming solves bit-identical at six (valid length, bucket) pairs, buckets up to 1,536 mel frames; PCC 0.9962–0.9988 against the fp32 torch reference (`scripts/perf_2026_09_25/`, `test_flow_decoder.py`) |
| Conv safety (`tt/hifigan/conv.py`, `upsample.py`) | Each new conv geometry is checked against a conservative path, with float64 arbitration on disagreement. This guards against silent conv errors, including #55545 | `test_conv1d_verification.py` |

### Results so far (N150, real weights)

- **End-to-end zero-shot synthesis** (non-streaming): WER 4.17% and speaker similarity 0.889 on one LibriSpeech prompt/target pair. On four more sentences, WER 0–2.8% and similarity 0.56–0.87 (Whisper `base.en`, CAM++ cosine).
- **RTF** 0.43–0.52 for 4.4–11.4 s of audio. This is the same request repeated in-process with the traced LLM decode and CFM (`use_decode_trace=True`, `COSYVOICE2_FLOW_CFM_TRACE=1`). Numbers on distinct utterances are pending.
- **Tests:** 160 collected, 159 pass on N150, 1 opt-in (allocation-tracker subprocess test).

### Not done yet

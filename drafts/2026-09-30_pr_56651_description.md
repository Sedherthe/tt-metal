Title: CosyVoice2 (#54104): TTNN bring-up on N150. Stage 1 targets met; streaming runs, Stage 3 targets measured and missed

### Summary

TTNN bring-up of [CosyVoice2-0.5B](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice2-0.5B) for #54104, in
`models/experimental/cosyvoice2/`. The whole model runs on an N150 (Wormhole): text → speech tokens (Qwen2 LLM) →
mel (flow matching) → 24 kHz waveform (HiFT vocoder).
- **Stage 1:** the four targets are met.
- **Streaming:** runs on upstream's chunk schedule and matches upstream's own streaming run.
- **Stage 3:** misses both targets. The flow's Euler steps are the lever, and they are what we're working on next.

It stays a draft until the Stage 3 work settles.

### The fix in this round: masked end padding in HiFT

Two kinds of HiFT call were padded at the end with silence mel, to a compiled bucket:
- streaming's final call (to 128 or 256 frames);
- Stage 1's single call for a mel under 512 frames (to 256 or 512).

The convs look ahead into that silence, so the last ~25 ms of every streamed utterance, and of three Stage 1
utterances, fell to −104 to −139 dBFS, where upstream's audio runs on at −52 to −89 dBFS. On 260-123440-0010, Whisper
decoded the silenced last 20 ms as "you" in 5 of 11 noise draws.

The fix (`tt/hifigan/valid_length.py`) keeps the bucket, so no geometry is added, but computes upstream's call at the
real length:
- the mel, every conv's output and each stage's sum are zeroed past the real length at their own rate;
- F0 is zeroed past the real frames;
- the source's 8 samples past the end are reflected, as `torch.stft` centering does;
- the STFT frames and the iSTFT magnitude past the end are zeroed;
- the last 3 samples take the real call's window normalization.

The evidence:
- **On the host** (`tests/pcc/test_hift_masked.py`, real checkpoint, 8 lengths): with the same F0, the masked padded
  call equals the real-length call within 1.2e-6.
- **On the device**, against HiFT compiled at each real length, the last 20 ms:
  - streaming's final calls: max |diff| ≤ 4.1e-4, PCC 0.9988–0.9999;
  - Stage 1's padded calls: max |diff| ≤ 4.9e-4, PCC 0.9987–0.9998.
- **The "you" clip:** no trailing "you" in any of 11 noise draws (5 of 11 before). A regression test renders the 11
  draws, and a reference-venv script transcribes them.
- **The cost:** 0.07 s per padded Stage 1 call at the 256-frame bucket and 0.10 s at 512. The streaming figures stay
  within the spread of the runs before the fix.
- **The gate that let it through.** The old tail gate had a −50 dBFS floor, which accepted the silenced ending. It is
  replaced by the level checks above, with no floor. They fail on the old padding and pass on the fix.

### Known issues

- **[#36487](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal/issues/36487): `prepare_conv_weights` gives wrong weights when
  `conv1d` slices its input through DRAM.**
  - Some HiFT and flow convs hit it at particular lengths.
  - Every new conv geometry is checked once against a raw-weight, safe-config reference, and a float64 host conv
    arbitrates. A wrong fast path never reaches the output.
  - Where the TILE-prepared weight is wrong, one prepared declaring a ROW_MAJOR input is usually right, and the
    checks keep it, so the conv stays traceable.
  - The checks cost 35 s of each warm start.
  - A comment with our geometries is drafted for #36487.
- **The kernel cache is reused only when a process allocates identically.**
  - With conv config tensors in DRAM (needed: in L1 they pile up in L1_SMALL, one set per geometry), the conv and
    halo reader kernels take those tensors' DRAM addresses as compile-time arguments.
  - A cached binary therefore matches only a process that allocates in the same order. Start-up runs a fixed warm-up
    sequence:
    - 3.1 min with the kernels on disk, 31.4 min on an empty cache;
    - the streaming set adds 2.5 and 13.0 min;
    - a request with no warm-up runs at RTF 66.1 on an empty cache.
  - An issue with a minimal reproducer is drafted, not filed. In this model, a 1 MiB tensor allocated first made an
    otherwise identical process recompile 1,132 of its warm-up's 2,373 binaries.
- **Streaming needs its own warm-up.** A chunk runs under the live decode trace, so `synthesize_stream` refuses to run
  before `warmup_streaming()`. Without it, the first chunk allocated 1,259 buffers under the trace.
- **Blackhole is untested.** A test guards the fused-SDPA tile-padding bug reported there (#57608). It passes on
  Wormhole, where the bug doesn't reproduce.
